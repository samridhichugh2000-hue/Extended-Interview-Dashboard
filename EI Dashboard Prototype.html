03

How the Worry Index is scored

Every signal carries a credit weight — minor ±0.5, average ±1, major ±2. Positive behaviour adds, negative deducts. The running total places the NJ in one of four bands.

Critical
score ≤ −4
PIP review triggered, HR intervenes this week.
Low
−3.5 to 0
PA candidate. Manager feedback requested early.
Medium
+0.5 to +5
Under watch, no action needed yet.
Good
> +5
On track for confirmation. Not to be monitored.

The weekly loop

One cron job on Monday morning drives the entire feedback cycle. No HR action required unless something breaks.

Asked of the new joiner
Asked of the manager
Rate the new joiner's overall effort and performance this week.
Satisfactory Average Below Expectations
Any tasks committed in the weekly update that were not completed? (open ended)
No response from the NJ by Monday EOD applies an auto shoddy. "Below Expectations" from a manager is AI-classified and deducts from the Worry Index.
